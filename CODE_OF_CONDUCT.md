# Code of conduct

Annulus follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be kind, assume good faith, and keep criticism on the work and not the person. Harassment of any kind isn't welcome here, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else the project meets.

If something goes wrong, please tell the maintainers privately through **Security → Report a vulnerability** on this repository, and say it's a conduct report. Reports are kept confidential, and the maintainers may warn, ban or remove anyone who doesn't follow this code.
